Intertia

RPGMaker, Blender, python, C, C++

Overview

A story focused turn based RPhttps://github.com/work-xinli525G. Involes combat with demons which are manifestation of human emotions, kind of like the demons in warhammer 40k. The main theme discusses how humans, despite knowing they are going down the wrong path, can not stop themselves from doubling down, like a force of nature is pushing them to keep making the same mistakes over and over. Kind of like, Interia.

Key Highlights

  • JRPG inspired combat: Traditional random encounter with attribute points distribution per level
  • Pyschological Horror: Linear story that explores the human psychology, and social commentary of the modern world
  • Choice-matters: Event and story choices that lead to different ending, pass judgement on the damned, and...yourself.
